# Bilingual site (Spanish + English)

Objective: the whole site usable in Spanish and English, language from the browser on first visit, switchable by hand.
Branch: `feat/i18n` · Delivery: work-unit commits on one branch, ff-merge to main · Tests: Vitest, `astro check`, `astro build`, browser check.

## Specs

- S1 — "en cuanto a lo del cambio de idioma de la web no veo donde cambiar el idioma la idea es que toda la web se pueda usar en espa;ol e ingles y pueda 1 tomar el idioma del navegador y que se pueda cambiar manualmente porfavor"
- S2 (assumption, design) — URLs carry the language: `/es/...` and `/en/...`. `/` picks the saved choice, else the browser language (English for `en*`, Spanish otherwise). A visible ES | EN switch in the header keeps the current page and remembers the choice.
- S3 (assumption) — "toda la web": UI copy, theory (topics, quizzes, practice bank), Cúmulo City (HUD, dialogs, missions), Service Duel content, profile and sign-in. Proper names of businesses may stay as they are.
- S4 (assumption) — Translations keep the same structure as Spanish (same ids, same right answers); a contract test compares both.
- S5 — Old unprefixed URLs keep working (redirect to the Spanish pages); `<html lang>`, hreflang alternates and the sitemap cover both languages.
